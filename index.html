Our Story

Pearls chosen with care, worn with meaning

Zarf Pearl brings together two pearl-growing traditions — the Akoya and South Sea waters of Japan, and the freshwater pearl farms of China — into a single collection for Bangladesh.

Every strand is checked by hand for lustre, shape, and colour before it reaches you. We keep our pieces understated by design: pearls that suit a wedding morning as easily as an ordinary Tuesday.

We're starting here in Bangladesh, with plans to bring Zarf Pearl to more of the region soon.
